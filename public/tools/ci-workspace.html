What this model includes

Feedstock production, land-use change, feedstock transport, process energy, process chemicals/fugitives, distribution, end-use emissions and signed credits. Direct terms are entered in gCO₂e/MJ; process inputs are multiplied by explicit emission factors. All quantities use an LHV energy basis. Enter direct terms after any allocation appropriate to your chosen method.

This scenario workspace uses the GBW engine snapshot supplied on 29 September 2026. Preset values are not newly verified emission-factor datasets. Regulatory methods require different allocation, credit, boundary and eligibility treatments; a shared scenario result is not a separate certified result for each program.
