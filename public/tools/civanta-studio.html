ImprovementCapital · USDAnnual fixed O&M · USD/yearApplied

Incremental LCFS value = (baseline CI − scenario CI) × eligible energy ÷ 10⁶ × USD/t. Annualized capital = capex × r / (1 − (1+r)−n). Net annual benefit = incremental LCFS value − annualized capital − change in O&M. Cost per tonne = (annualized capital + O&M change) ÷ annual avoided tonnes. These screening assumptions do not establish eligibility, feasibility or financing.

Sensitivity coefficients come from this screening prototype. For production, each scenario must rerun the pathway-specific LCA engine; the Optimizer would then evaluate all combinations, annualized cost and target feasibility.

Step 4 · Credit planning

What could this scenario be worth?

Three separate programmes, three separate eligibility tests. Nothing here is an issued credit, and a modelled CI does not establish approval under any of them.

RIN quantity follows eligible volume and the approved equivalence value, not your CI. Lowering CI does not create more RINs.

For liquid fuels, indicative RINs = eligible US gallons × equivalence value. For eligible RNG, quantity is based on eligible LHV energy at 77,000 Btu per RIN. Gross value = quantity × assumed price, which is not incremental optimization revenue.
